The direct C++ suite tests field validation, date/leap-year boundaries, score
boundaries, group and student operations, duplicate names and stable IDs,
all edited fields, group transfers, renaming groups, cascading deletions,
escaped text and exact numeric round trips, ID continuity after restarting,
failed saves, recovery-file protection, CRLF files, and malformed files.

The end-to-end suite tests the compiled program through its public console:
creating and displaying records, editing every field, group transfers and
renames, empty edit defaults, invalid menu responses and record selections,
invalid edits, cancelled and confirmed deletions, interrupted input, restart
persistence, score boundaries, sample data, startup failures and save failures.
Rejected changes, cancelled deletions and interrupted operations are checked
against the bytes of the previously saved file.

Machine-readable console results and captured sessions are in test_results.json
(Windows) and test_results_linux.json (Linux). Both files include SHA-256 hashes
of the five application source/header files tested.
